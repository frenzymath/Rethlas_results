# theorem thm:BS.6.1

## statement
Let $k$ be a field. Let $\mathfrak{H}$ be the Heisenberg Lie algebra with basis $\{x, y, z\}$ and multiplication $[x,y] = z$, $[x,z] = [y,z] = 0$, graded by $\deg(x) = \deg(y) = 1$, $\deg(z) = 2$. Given any Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$, its universal enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{g})$ is the tensor algebra on $\mathfrak{g}$ modulo the ideal generated by $x \otimes y - y \otimes x - [x,y]$. It is easy to see that every integral point on a pure ray in the Boij--S\"oderberg cone of dimension $\leq 3$ is realizable over either $k[x,y,z]$ or $U(\mathfrak{H})$.

[Source: D. Erman and S. V. Sam, Questions about Boij--S\"oderberg theory, arXiv:1606.01867, 2016.]

For every degree sequence $(d_0, d_1, d_2, d_3)$, and every integral point on the corresponding ray in the Boij--S\"oderberg cone, is there a finite length module either over $k[x,y,z]$ or $U(\mathfrak{H})$ whose Betti table is that integral point?

## proof
No.

Take the degree sequence
\[
(d_0,d_1,d_2,d_3)=(0,6,20,21).
\]
The corresponding codimension-$3$ pure Herzog--Kuhl ratios are
\[
\frac{1}{6\cdot 20\cdot 21}:\frac{1}{6\cdot 14\cdot 15}:\frac{1}{20\cdot 14\cdot 1}:\frac{1}{21\cdot 15\cdot 1}
=1:2:9:8.
\]
So
\[
\beta=(1,2,9,8)
\]
is an integral point on that pure ray.

We show that \(\beta\) is realizable over neither \(k[x,y,z]\) nor \(U(\mathfrak H)\).

First let \(S=k[x,y,z]\). If a finite length graded \(S\)-module \(M\) had Betti table \(\beta\), then \(\beta_0(M)=1\), so \(M\cong S/I\) for some homogeneous ideal \(I\). Also \(\beta_1(M)=2\), so \(I\) is generated by two elements. Hence \(\operatorname{ht}(I)\le 2\) by the Krull height theorem. Therefore \(\dim(S/I)\ge 1\), contradicting the assumption that \(M\) has finite length. Thus \(\beta\) is not realizable over \(k[x,y,z]\).

Now let \(A=U(\mathfrak H)\). By the Poincare--Birkhoff--Witt theorem, the monomials \(x^ay^bz^c\) form a graded \(k\)-basis of \(A\), where \(\deg(x)=\deg(y)=1\) and \(\deg(z)=2\). Hence
\[
H_A(t)=\sum_{a,b,c\ge 0} t^{a+b+2c}=\frac{1}{(1-t)^2(1-t^2)}.
\]
If a finite length graded \(A\)-module \(N\) had Betti table \(\beta\), then
\[
H_N(t)=\frac{1-2t^6+9t^{20}-8t^{21}}{(1-t)^2(1-t^2)}.
\]
Because \(N\) has finite length, \(H_N(t)\) is a polynomial. Therefore the numerator
\[
P(t)=1-2t^6+9t^{20}-8t^{21}
\]
must be divisible by \((1-t)^2(1-t^2)\), and in particular by \(1+t\). But
\[
P(-1)=1-2+9+8=16\neq 0.
\]
So \(P(t)\) is not divisible by \(1+t\), contradiction. Thus \(\beta\) is not realizable over \(U(\mathfrak H)\).

Therefore the integral point \((1,2,9,8)\) on the pure ray for \((0,6,20,21)\) is realizable over neither \(k[x,y,z]\) nor \(U(\mathfrak H)\). Hence the answer to the question is negative. ∎
